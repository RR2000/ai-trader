What this is

An experiment from April 2023: a deep Q-network (two LSTM layers) trained with reinforcement learning on one-minute Bitcoin candles to choose buy, sell or hold. It was trained on a very small slice of data, so do not expect it to be clever.

Every minute this service reads the latest closed BTC/USDT candles from Binance's public market data, builds the same input the model was trained on (30 minutes of price, volume, SMA 10, SMA 30, RSI, MACD and its signal, min-max scaled) and applies the model's choice to a pretend portfolio that started with 1,000 USDT: all in or all out, 0.1% fee per trade, no slippage. The comparison line bought BTC with the same 1,000 USDT at the same moment, paying one fee, and never sold.

No real money is involved and nothing here is financial advice. Source code: github.com/RR2000/ai-trader.
