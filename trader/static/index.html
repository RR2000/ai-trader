What this is

Whenever a candle closes (each simulation has its own candle length, from one minute to one hour), the service reads it from Binance's public market data and lets the simulation decide: buy, sell or hold. The decision is applied to a pretend portfolio with the same accounting for all of them: the fee taken on every trade (0.1% by default), no slippage, everything recorded. Return is the change in value since the simulation started; vs buy and hold is that return minus what buying the coin at the same first candle would have made, in percentage points.

The simulations are defined in a file in the repository; open one for its chart, its trades and how it decides.

No real money is involved and nothing here is financial advice. Source code: github.com/RR2000/ai-trader.
