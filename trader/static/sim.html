How it decides

 

Every simulation uses the same accounting: a pretend portfolio, the fee taken on every trade, no slippage. Its buy and hold line spent the same cash on the coin at the same first candle, paying one fee, and never sold.

No real money is involved and nothing here is financial advice. Source code: github.com/RR2000/ai-trader.
